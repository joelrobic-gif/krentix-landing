How it works

Most coding agents give you their first answer. Krentix gives you the best answer — verified before you ever see it.

  1. 01.

    Krentix engineers your question first. Then routes it to the right team.

    Before any AI sees your prompt, Krentix rewrites it for maximum yield — injecting context, prescribing format, routing to the right modality. You write naturally. The model receives a precision-engineered prompt. If two targeted questions would get a dramatically better answer, Krentix asks them — never more.

  2. 02.

    Your answer is checked independently — before it reaches you.

    Krentix doesn't ask the same AI that produced your answer to also check it. A second, separate review runs without access to the first. Disagreement between the two is the signal — it catches blind spots, overconfidence, and hallucinations before they reach you.

  3. 03.

    Only verified answers ship. If it fails the check, you never see it.

    Answers that fail are retried and corrected automatically. You see only what passed. If you want to know what was checked and why — the full audit trail is one click away.
