03 · Positioning

Why Krentix is different

Not about how many models. About models that can’t cover for each other.

LLM aggregators pick a model and hand you its answer. Krentix runs independent specialists who haven’t seen each other’s reasoning — then forces reconciliation. The benchmark gains come from disagreement surfacing what agreement would hide. A model that missed an edge case can’t hide behind consensus when an independent verifier flags it. Every model that contributes to your answer is accountable to the others — and none can vouch for itself.

When one model generates and the same model checks, the reviewer shares the generator’s blind spots, its overconfidence, its hallucinations. That’s why Claude Code, Cursor, and Copilot all plateau at the same ceiling — the conflict of interest is structural. Krentix scores above every frontier model on the same public benchmarks not because it has more models, but because no model in the ensemble is allowed to grade its own work.

Cost-per-quality, not cost-per-token. Provenance, not vibes.

Most agents bill the maximum model on every keystroke. Krentix automatically uses the smallest model that produces a verified answer — and escalates only when verification demands it. An order-of-magnitude lower aggregate cost than always-on frontier-only tooling. Every answer carries a provenance trail — which checks passed, which were overruled, and why.
