How it works

One pipeline, twelve cross-checks.

Every request runs through specialised exit branches: tool use, council debate, vision panel, constitutional refusal review, crystal cache, instinct match, full ensemble. The cheapest route that solves the question wins. The expensive routes only fire when they have to.

01

Triage

Constitutional ensemble screens for sensitive intent before any model runs.

02

Pipeline picks the cheapest exit branch that can solve the question with high confidence.

03

Twelve verifiers cross-check the candidate. Disagreement triggers escalation, not silence.

04

Ship

Answer streams under two seconds. Provenance trail logged. Cost recorded against the daily cap.

Questions

Often asked.

Why is it so much cheaper?

Krentix routes the cheapest free-tier provider that can answer with verifier consensus (Cerebras, Groq, Google Gemini Flash, OpenRouter). Paid Sonnet only fires as a fallback, gated at $2 per day.

Does my data leave my machine?

Only the provider call you intentionally route through. Persistent memory, audit logs, crystal cache, and provenance trail stay on your disk in ~/.krentix/. Constitutional refusals are auditable, local, and signed.

How do twelve verifiers actually disagree?

Each verifier has a domain, a model, and a constitution. Disagreement above threshold escalates: more verifiers, paid fallback, or a constitutional vote. Silent agreement is logged the same way as dissent.

What happens when the bridge is offline?

Krentix is a Windows service (krentixbridge.exe) that auto-restarts. The UI surfaces an honest "bridge offline" state with a correlation id. No fake answers, no fallback prompts.

Can I see what each verifier voted?

Yes — every answer carries a provenance trail. Click "details" under any answer to see model, latency, cost, and dissent.

Is it open source?

The bridge, pipeline, and verifier orchestration live at github.com/joelrobic-gif/krentix. Constitutional ensemble weights are user-tunable.

What does it cost to run on my machine?

$0 in subscription. $0.20–$0.60 per day in pay-as-you-go provider fees, hard-capped at $2/day. No floor — quiet days cost nothing.

What about data retention and audit?

Provenance is local-first JSONL. Export to NDJSON via /api/audit/export. Nothing reports home.
